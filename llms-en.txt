## What iiRest Does

- Presents the iiRest product line for restaurant management on the homepage.
- Highlights the ready AI assistant for managers: it connects to iiRest data, understands restaurant context, answers questions, and prepares reports.
- Compares supplier prices in one matrix on `/zakupki/`.
- Speeds up procurement order preparation and helps identify overpayment.
- Supports price-list uploads, supplier website workflows, and purchasing analytics.
- Prepares future agents for cafe marketers and employee-style routine tasks; visitors can leave a request on the homepage.

## Primary Audience
